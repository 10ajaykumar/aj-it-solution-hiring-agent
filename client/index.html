Coverage depends on each vendor's API and your ATS plan. Click a capability to run it in the ATS Console.

QuestionsWhat HR teams ask

Straight answers about AJ.

Does AJ replace recruiters?

No. It takes over the admin: searching, screening, scheduling and ATS updates. Every shortlist, outreach message and offer still needs a recruiter's approval, so recruiters spend more time with candidates and hiring managers.

Is this a live product?

This is a working demo on sample data. The scoring in Try it yourself and Recruiter vs AJ is real, and Live AI answers come from a real AI model when it is switched on. ATS connections and job-board searches are illustrated, not live.

Which ATS can it work with?

Any ATS with an API: Workday, SuccessFactors, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, Darwinbox, Zoho Recruit and SmartRecruiters among them. Your ATS stays the record of truth and AJ writes updates back to it.

How is candidate data protected?

Name, gender and age are masked during scoring. Every agent action is logged with its reason. The design keeps data in India and follows DPDP Act 2023 rules for consent and retention.

What would a pilot look like?

Four weeks on three to five live requisitions, measured on time-to-fill, recruiter hours saved and hiring-manager satisfaction against a control group.
